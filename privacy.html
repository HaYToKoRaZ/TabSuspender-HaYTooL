Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

TabSuspender HaYTooL is built with privacy as a foundational principle. The extension operates completely client-side, collects zero personal data, and performs zero remote telemetry.

1. Data Collection and Usage

We believe your browsing activities are strictly your personal business. TabSuspender HaYTooL:

  • Does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Does not track browsing history, visited web addresses (URLs), or page content.
  • Does not transmit any data to external servers, cloud endpoints, or third-party analytics services.
  • Does not sell, rent, or monetize user data under any circumstances.

2. Local Storage and Preferences

The extension requires permission to store your local preferences (such as suspension timeouts, whitelisted domains, and theme choices). All such configuration settings are stored exclusively in your local application storage on your own device. This data never leaves your computer unless you explicitly export or sync it manually.

3. Permissions Overview

TabSuspender HaYTooL requests only the absolute minimum permissions strictly necessary to execute its primary utility:

  • Tabs & Active Tab: Used solely to detect idle background tabs and perform local tab suspension/restoration. Page contents are never read or stored.
  • Storage: Used exclusively to preserve your personal settings and whitelist rules locally on your device.
  • Context Menus: Used to provide convenient right-click shortcuts (e.g., "Suspend this tab", "Whitelist domain").
  • Alarms: Used to trigger periodic local idle timer evaluations in the background.

4. Third-Party Services

TabSuspender HaYTooL contains no third-party advertisements, tracking pixels, cookies, or analytical SDKs. No communication is initiated with any third-party infrastructure during standard operation.

5. Changes to This Policy

Any future updates to this Privacy Policy will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date. Since we do not collect contact details, users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
